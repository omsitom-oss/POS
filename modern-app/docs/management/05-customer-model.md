# Customer model

`CustomerId` is the stable internal primary key for the lifetime of a customer. `PublicId` is a unique GUID for external routes and integrations; `CustomerCode` is a unique human-readable reference generated by the backend with cryptographically secure random characters. None derives from the business name, license, deployment or database assignment.

Business name and contact details are descriptive data. Business names are not unique because distinct customers may use the same legal or trading name. The current create form requires a non-empty business name and one active primary business type; contact fields are optional. Customer creation writes `Customers` and `CustomerBusinessTypes` in one transaction. It does not issue a license or create a deployment.

`CustomerBusinessTypes` is many-to-many with a composite primary key that prevents duplicate assignment. A filtered unique index permits at most one primary business type. The create endpoint creates exactly one primary assignment. Additional business-type management is future work.

Customer statuses are `ACTIVE`, `INACTIVE` and `SUSPENDED`; new customers start `ACTIVE`. No customer delete endpoint exists, and foreign keys prevent accidental deletion while dependent history remains.

Business type codes are database data with a unique constraint. The initial codes are `PHARMACY`, `SUPERMARKET`, `ELECTRONICS`, `GENERAL_RETAIL` and `WHOLESALE`. Add future business types as rows, not code branches or separate builds.
